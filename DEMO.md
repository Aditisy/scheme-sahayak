# Three-minute demo script

**Status:** run only after `index/` is built and the app is deployed.

1. **Problem (20 seconds):** “A student or rural family needs to know whether they qualify, what they receive, and what documents they need. The answer is buried in long official PDFs, so Scheme Sahayak searches those sources directly.”
2. Ask: “How much assistance does PM-KISAN provide?” Expand Sources and show the file and page.
3. Ask: “Who can receive PM-KISAN benefits?”
4. Follow up: “And what documents do I need for that?” Show that the conversation context informs the rewrite and inspect sources.
5. Ask: “Who won the 2023 cricket world cup?” Show the amber not-found badge and exact refusal.
6. Open Sources to show a cited passage, then `eval/results.md` for measured retrieval results and a real failure case.

Do not present this demo as complete until index creation, the evaluation audit, and public deployment have succeeded.
